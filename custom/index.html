Choose the right custom material.

If you are not sure whether PC, ABS or PP is better, we compare target price, print effect, durability, weight, and sales channel before recommending a direction.

PC custom luggage

Best for high-gloss printed shell effects, photo artwork, gift programs, and products that need a premium visual impression.

  • Good for scenery, portrait, brand artwork and high-resolution print.
  • Works well for custom pattern collections and limited editions.
  • Recommended when the shell image is the main selling point.

ABS custom luggage

Best for cost-sensitive programs that still need strong retail color, logo, packaging, and accessory matching.

  • Good for color series, zipper luggage, logo placement and hardware matching.
  • Useful for supermarket, online retail and promotional product lines.
  • Future ABS cases can show color matching and shell texture examples.

PP custom luggage

Best for durable, lightweight, practical travel products where shell structure and long-term use matter more than full-surface printing.

  • Good for solid color, family luggage sets, wheel and trolley matching.
  • Can support brand logo, interior lining, carton mark and retail packaging.
  • Future PP cases can show shell color and hardware combinations.

What you can prepare.

These details help us quote faster and prepare a sample-ready direction with less back-and-forth.

Artwork requirements

  • High-resolution JPG/PNG for photo printing, preferably clear and large enough for suitcase size.
  • AI, PDF, PSD, or vector logo files for brand marks and packaging.
  • Pantone or reference color if you need exact color matching.
  • Confirm whether artwork can be used commercially before production.

Order information

  • Material preference: PC, ABS, PP, or mixed material structure.
  • Target size: 20, 24, 26, 28 inch, or full luggage set.
  • Quantity, destination country, target price level, and expected delivery time.
  • Logo, lining, wheel, trolley, lock, carton mark, and packaging requirements.

Common custom order questions.

These answers reduce back-and-forth before quotation and help you understand what can be confirmed immediately and what needs a sample.

Can you send only a photo or rough idea?

Yes. A clear photo, reference product, brand color, or rough concept is enough to begin discussion. If the image is not suitable for printing, we will ask for a higher-resolution file or help define a better layout direction.

Can the same artwork be used on different sizes?

Usually yes, but the artwork layout may need adjustment for 20, 24, 26, and 28 inch shells because each size has a different printable area and curve. We recommend confirming the main size first, then adapting other sizes.

What affects the sample cost and sample time?

Material, shell mold, print method, logo method, accessory color, packaging, and whether existing parts can be used all affect sample cost and timing. A simple logo or color change is faster than a full-surface print or new structure.

Can you customize wheels, trolley and lock?

Yes. Wheels, trolley, handle, lock, zipper, puller, lining, and carton mark can be discussed. Some parts have ready colors, while special colors may require higher quantity or longer preparation.

Can you help choose the cheapest shipping method?

We can help compare practical shipping options based on destination, quantity, carton volume, urgency, and your preference. For bulk orders, sea freight is often more economical; samples usually ship by courier or air.

What should be confirmed before mass production?

The final sample standard, artwork, material, size, quantity, logo, accessories, packaging, carton mark, inspection points, delivery method, and payment terms should be confirmed before bulk production starts.
